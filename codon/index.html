CAT — His

Built by people who were tired of
spreadsheets full of FASTA files.

CODON started in a university sequencing core in 2021, where three computational biologists kept rebuilding the same pipeline for every new project — QC here, variant calling there, structure prediction bolted on with a script nobody wanted to maintain. We built the tool we wished we'd had, then kept building it for the labs around us.

Today CODON runs behind the scenes at academic sequencing cores, biotech startups, and a handful of larger R&D teams who needed their pipeline to keep up with their sequencer. The interface changed. The obsession with getting the reading frame right, base by base, never did.

"We stopped asking people to trust a black box, and started showing them the frame it read the sequence in."

TGG — Trp

What ships in the pipeline.

  • SEQ

    Sequence QC & assembly

    Automated quality control, adapter trimming, and de novo assembly, held to lab-grade thresholds you set once.

  • VAR

    Variant calling & annotation

    Call variants against a reference or de novo, annotated against curated clinical and functional databases.

  • DES

    Construct design & validation

    Design synthetic constructs, screen for off-target effects, and simulate expression before you order synthesis.

  • FLD

    Structure & function prediction

    Predict 3D folds and binding pockets, ranked by model confidence so you know where to look first.

  • LIM

    Lab & LIMS integration

    Sync directly with your sequencer and lab inventory system — samples in, no manual re-entry.

  • RPT

    Compliance-ready reporting

    Exportable, audit-ready reports formatted for institutional review and regulatory submission.
