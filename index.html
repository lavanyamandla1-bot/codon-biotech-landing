Our thesis

Every disease is an
information problem.

For sixty years, drug discovery has meant screening millions of molecules and hoping one works. CODON starts from the other direction: the genome is a program, and disease is what happens when that program runs incorrectly.

We design molecules the way engineers design software — specified, versioned, and tested against a known target — because a therapy that's designed is faster to build and easier to trust than one that's merely discovered.

// discovered

  • Screen millions of candidates
  • Iterate by trial and error
  • Years to a validated lead

// designed

  • Model the target directly
  • Simulate before synthesizing
  • Weeks to a validated lead

The platform

From sequence to therapy,
in four calls.

Every CODON program moves through the same disciplined pipeline — a fixed process built for precision that scales, not one-off heroics.

  1. 01

    Sequence Design

    Our design engine models millions of candidate RNA sequences against a therapeutic target, optimizing for stability, expression, and specificity before a single base is synthesized.

  2. 02

    Codon Optimization

    We tune the genetic code itself — selecting synonymous codons that maximize protein expression in human tissue without changing the amino acid sequence they encode.

  3. 03

    Delivery Engineering

    Lipid nanoparticles are formulated and iterated in parallel with the sequence, so payload and vehicle are designed together — never bolted on after the fact.

  4. 04

    Manufacturing at Scale

    Programs that clear design and delivery move directly into GMP-ready synthesis, carrying the same specification from simulation through to production.
